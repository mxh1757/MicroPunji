Built around four principles

Most of what determines an outcome is decided before a single dollar is deployed: how a mandate is structured, how positions are sized, and how often a client actually sees what's happening inside their own portfolio. MicroPunji is organized around getting that structure right first.

  • Diversification

    Capital is spread across equities, digital assets, metals, and indices, not concentrated in a single narrative.

  • Discipline

    Allocation rules and rebalancing triggers are agreed in advance, not improvised in reaction to headlines.

  • Transparency

    Every mandate comes with a defined reporting cadence, so clients always know their current positions.

  • Access

    Higher tiers unlock broader asset access and more direct advisor contact, not different levels of honesty.

How a mandate comes together

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We discuss your goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance before recommending a tier or a strategy.

  2. 02

    Allocation

    We design a mandate across the asset classes appropriate to your tier and risk profile.

  3. 03

    Execution

    Capital is deployed according to the agreed strategy, with position sizing set in advance.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    You receive statements on your tier's reporting cadence, plus scheduled portfolio reviews.
